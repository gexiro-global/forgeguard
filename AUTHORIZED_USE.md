# Authorized Use

VersionSec by Gexiro is for defensive posture self-checks.

You may use VersionSec only against instances you own or are explicitly authorized to assess.

You must not use VersionSec for internet-wide discovery, third-party probing, or activity that exceeds your authorization.

You must not modify VersionSec to request package blobs, manifests, or other protected artifacts from third-party systems.

Do not treat VersionSec output as proof of compromise without independent verification. VersionSec v0.2 performs posture inference, not exploitation.
